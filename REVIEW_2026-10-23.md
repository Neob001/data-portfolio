## 2. What worked / what didn't

Worked:
- Jobs data from public ATS boards: the only market we entered where demand is large AND legally reachable.
- Coverage: Workday (+385k jobs), Personio, Teamtailor -> index 808,862 jobs / 19,357 boards (Oct 22), built nightly in 112 of 140 min with 0 boards skipped.
- Reliability fixes (publish gap, bad URLs, two OOM causes): Jobs Feed failure rate 20% -> ~0% on new runs (30-day figure 5% and falling).
- Store search rank follows popularity: Jobs Feed is #4 for "lever jobs" and "ashby jobs".

Didn't:
- Official-data niches (SEC, TED, OFAC, VAT...): tiny demand, zero users after 6 weeks.
- One-Actor-per-keyword (Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby/Workday Jobs API): 0 users after 2 weeks; new Actors stay buried until they have users.
- Website tools (lighthouse, screenshot, tech-stack): 1 paying user in Sept, none since.

## 4. Strategy changes

1. **Focus: jobs only.** Every hour goes to the jobs cluster (Jobs Feed first). No new non-jobs Actors; other Actors stay live untouched (they cost nothing, auto-test keeps them healthy).
2. **Price (done):** Jobs Feed $3 -> $5/1k from 2026-11-06 (P2). Watch paying users and runs for 2 weeks after; roll back only if paying users drop.
3. **Visibility in the big pools (L2, needs approval):** Jobs Feed's listing never says "job listings" or "job postings" - the words of the 3,160-user and 5,951-user pools. Add them to the SEO title and the start of the description; keep the visible title (it already ranks #4 for lever/ashby).
4. **Coverage (R1, needs approval):** our robots rule is stricter than the standard (RFC 9309: a missing robots.txt = no restrictions). It blocks iCIMS and Oracle Recruiting Cloud entirely and ~300 Teamtailor boards. Adopting RFC 9309 for 4xx (keep HTML/5xx/unreachable = disallow, keep every explicit Disallow) reopens them. SAP SuccessFactors stays out (explicit Disallow: /).
5. **Retention of paying users:** make Jobs Feed the easiest daily feed: example tasks for scheduled "new jobs since last run" feeds (Make/Zapier/Sheets) - no listing change needed.
6. **Measure weekly:** paying users, Jobs Feed users/runs, rank for the 8 searches above, index jobs/boards, failure rate.
